Why this model works

Built from real mentorship experience.

The model comes from programs run at Salomon Brothers, Citigroup, EZOPS, RiskFocus, and Ness—taking engineers with 0–2 years of experience and developing them into billable, client-ready professionals through 3–5 months of structured training.

The gap

Resume and LinkedIn polish are useful, but hiring managers need something more important: proof that you can do the work.

Capstone + network

Each cycle ends with a capstone presentation for engineers, managers, and industry contacts—turning real work into real opportunities.
