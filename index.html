03 / Why this model works

Built from real mentorship programs.

This model was shaped at Salomon Brothers, Citigroup, EZOPS, RiskFocus, and Ness—where structured training turned early-career engineers into client-ready professionals.

The track record

0–2 years experience → fully billable, client-ready engineers

Three to five months of structured training produced engineers who could actually deliver in real environments. This was not résumé coaching.

The gap

Helping with résumés and LinkedIn is useful—but it does not get people hired. Hiring managers want proof you can do the work.

Capstone + network

Each cycle ends with a capstone presentation for engineers, managers, and industry contacts. The goal: turn real work into real opportunities.
